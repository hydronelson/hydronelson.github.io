## Research interests & methodologies

**CyanoHAB remote sensing** — tracking cyanobacterial harmful algal blooms in regional source water reservoirs using public-domain satellite constellations (Sentinel-3 OLCI, Sentinel-2 MSI, Landsat 8/9) via the EPA Cyanobacteria Assessment Network (CyAN); spectral analysis targeting phycocyanin absorption (620 nm) and chlorophyll-a to map bloom migration relative to municipal intake towers before raw water quality degrades.

**Open-source hydraulic modeling** — extended-period simulations in EPANET with open municipal/USGS GIS datasets: pressure zones, velocity distributions, and water age in looped distribution networks; simulating transient pressure waves and backflow contaminant travel paths to locate high-hazard vulnerability points.

**SCADA historian analytics** — SQL and Python queries over continuous time-series feeds: correlating raw turbidity spikes and pH shifts against coagulant feed rates, filter head loss curves, and disinfectant residual decay (CT calculations); forensic event tracing for audit-proof operational logs.

**Cross-connection program architecture** — containment assembly failure rates and hazard-assessment frameworks under state drinking water regulations and the SDWA; defensible compliance monitoring protocols for high-hazard commercial and industrial risks.
